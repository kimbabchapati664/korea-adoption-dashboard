Roots Finder · beta

Tell it what you have. It tells you what it means.

Tick what is in your folder, or paste the text from your papers and let the Finder pick things out. You get what each item means for your search, the Korean words to search with, and a roadmap in the right order.

Private by design. The Finder runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or tick is sent anywhere or saved. Close the tab and it is gone. It is a rule-based guide built from official sources, not legal advice.
