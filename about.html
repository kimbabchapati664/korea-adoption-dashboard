About me

Hi, I'm HJ Kim.

"Nobody should need money, or Korean, to ask where they came from."

I live in Seoul and run a small company here called All Things Asia. This site, and the time I give to adoptees, is volunteer work.

More than 168,000 Korean children were sent overseas for adoption. Many of them are adults now, coming back with a thin folder of papers in a language they never had the chance to learn, into a system that confuses even Koreans.

I speak the language, I can pick up the phone, and I know how the offices here work. For me, reading a document or making a call takes an hour. For someone searching from Minnesota or Stockholm, the same thing can take months, or never happen at all.

So I started doing it for free, and I built this site so the first steps are open to everyone, whether or not we ever meet.

How I work

Three promises

Free comes first

Your records request, your file review and your police DNA registration are free through NCRC and the Korean police. I will always point you there first. I will never sell you access to your own file.

Honest about the odds

Many files are thin, and some are wrong. I will tell you plainly what your paperwork does and does not make possible, before you spend anything or get your hopes up.

Your story stays yours

Nothing identifiable about you or your birth family goes on this site or anywhere else without your explicit permission.

Where I can help

As a volunteer, when time allows

Reading your papers

Going through Korean documents with you and explaining what they actually say.

Calls in Korean

Phoning NCRC, an agency, a hospital or a children's home when English is not getting you anywhere.

Your first request

Helping you prepare the Adoption Information Disclosure Request so nothing is missed.

The right people

Pointing you to the adoptee-led groups and services that fit your situation.
