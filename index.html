Three things to know first

Before you spend a single dollar

Your records are free

Since 19 July 2025 every Korean adoption file is held by NCRC, the National Center for the Rights of the Child. Requesting your information, reviewing your file and registering your DNA with the Korean police cost nothing.

You can start from abroad

The records request is filed online, and the police DNA sample can be given at a Korean embassy or consulate. You do not need to fly to Korea to begin.

Thin files are normal

Many files contain very little, and some contain errors. That is a documented pattern, not your failure. DNA and public search boards exist exactly for this.

A warning worth repeating. NCRC has stated that private homeland-visit programs are not affiliated with it. Nobody can sell you "access" to your own file. If someone asks you to pay for something on the list above, walk away.
